Read `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/DESIGN_STRATEGY.md`, and `docs/PROJECT_STATE.md` first.

Then:

1. Confirm which approved service page is next.
2. Preserve the existing Acton shell and design tokens.
3. Reuse the Tire & Wheel content-page structure where it fits.
4. Identify one topic-specific module for the new service.
5. Use only approved copy and facts; flag missing content instead of inventing it.
6. Keep one H1 and a logical SEO heading hierarchy.
7. Check desktop, tablet, and mobile behavior.
8. Update `docs/PROJECT_STATE.md` after the coherent milestone.

Do not add AAN integration and do not deploy.
