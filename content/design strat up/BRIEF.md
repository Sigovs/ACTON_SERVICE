# Project brief

## Project

Acton Autowerks — six service landing pages for Acton, Massachusetts.

## Current phase

Foundation and discovery. The global website identity already exists, but no service-page design has been approved and no page should yet be treated as the internal-page baseline.

## Business position supported by the copy

An independent automotive service business with more than 20 years of combined experience, a strong emphasis on European vehicles, transparent advice, and service for Acton, MetroWest, and New England drivers.

This is a working interpretation of the supplied copy, not permission to invent additional business claims.

## Audience

- Owners of European vehicles seeking a knowledgeable independent specialist.
- Local Acton and MetroWest drivers seeking routine maintenance or focused repair.
- Performance and enthusiast owners who care about correct equipment, diagnosis, and parts.
- Drivers who value clear explanations and do not want unnecessary upselling.

## Primary job of every page

Build enough trust and clarity for a qualified visitor to schedule an appointment or call 978-429-8913.

## Confirmed project context

- Acton hired another company to design and launch the current website.
- The live site is missing six service pages recovered from the previous site.
- The copy was approved on 2026-08-12 and the task is explicitly “need 6 pages designed.”
- The working estimate allocates 2 design hours and 2 build hours per page because approved/recovered copy is supplied.

## Scope

Six separate pages:

1. Tire and Wheel Service in Acton, MA
2. Maintenance and Service Intervals in Acton, MA
3. European Car Repair Specialists
4. Electrical Systems Diagnostics & Service in Acton, MA
5. Auto Body Services in Acton, MA
6. Transmission Service in Acton, MA

The scope is six internal page designs and static front-end mockups that fit the current website. The homepage, header, footer, and global visual identity are context to preserve, not redesign scope.

## Shared content pattern

Five focused pages broadly share:

- Service-specific introduction
- Service list or capability overview
- Education/signs/why-it-matters section
- Trust explanation
- FAQ
- Appointment CTA and phone number

This is a content pattern, not permission to duplicate one layout six times.

## Exceptional page

The European Car Repair page is a long-form service hub covering seven brands:

- Audi
- BMW
- Mercedes-Benz
- Porsche
- Land Rover
- Jaguar
- Volkswagen

It needs brand-level navigation, progressive disclosure, or another clear browsing model. A single uninterrupted wall of text is not acceptable, but all supplied content must remain reachable and indexable.

## Conversion model

- Primary: Schedule an Appointment / Schedule Service
- Secondary: Call 978-429-8913
- Contextual: Explore a service or European brand section

Avoid multiple competing filled CTAs in the same view.

## Missing inputs to collect before final design

- Current approved logo files and brand marks
- Access to the current site's final design and asset files
- Approved typefaces or existing font files
- Approved brand colors, if any
- Real facility, technician, diagnostic, wheel/tire, and service photography
- Review-source permission and exact 4.9-star substantiation
- Exact schedule-service URL/form behavior
- Header/footer requirements from the live site
- Confirmation that all copy in the PDF is approved for publication
- Legal approval for manufacturer names, logos, and any trademark-sensitive language

## Deliverable path

1. Current-site design audit and focused research/contact sheet
2. Two or three compatible service-page territories
3. One approved focused service-page baseline inside the current shell
5. Remaining four focused pages
6. European Car Repair hub
7. Responsive and accessibility QA
8. Confirmed-platform implementation and developer handoff

## Acceptance criteria

- Every supplied page is unmistakably separate and correctly titled.
- The system feels coherent without producing six cloned pages.
- The European hub remains navigable despite its length.
- The pages clearly belong to the current Acton Autowerks site while improving internal-page hierarchy beyond a generic dark luxury template.
- Copy is traceable to the source and no claims are invented.
- Desktop and mobile preserve the same hierarchy and visual idea.
- The approved visual system is documented clearly for the later implementation handoff.
