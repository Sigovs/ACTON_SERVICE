# Current site context

Live site: https://www.actonautowerks.com/

## What is fixed for this assignment

- Existing header, utility bar, navigation, logo, footer, and overall site identity
- Unbounded for display/headings
- Montserrat for body and interface text
- Black/white/cool-gray foundation
- Blue-teal primary action color: `#0B5C7F` observed on the live Get A Quote button
- Existing service, performance, detailing, and protection positioning

## What the six-page assignment adds

- A coherent internal service-page grammar
- Better long-form hierarchy and local navigation
- Page-specific visual stories for tires, maintenance, European repair, electrical systems, body coordination, and transmission
- Shared FAQ and conversion behavior
- A large European Repair hub capable of containing seven brand chapters

## Strong source material already present

The live site and its Instagram feed contain real subject matter that is more specific than generic luxury-car marketing:

- Porsche 996 wheel fitment, alignment, maintenance, and paint correction
- Mercedes-AMG C63 60k service work
- Land Rover scheduled service
- European cars on lifts in the actual shop
- Technician and diagnostic/service photography
- Customer reviews emphasizing equal respect for ordinary and six-figure vehicles, honest advice, and no pressure

This material should inform the new pages and may provide usable assets once final-file access is obtained.

## Current-site weaknesses not to propagate blindly

- Generic luxury-service headlines and repetitive marketing claims
- Large black hero space with minimal service evidence
- Repeated Get A Quote CTAs without enough contextual distinction
- Decorative automotive/tech line art that does not explain the service
- Percentage/rating and generic benefit modules that need factual and visual clarification

Continuity does not require repeating these weaknesses. Keep the identity anchors while improving service-specific hierarchy, evidence, and page rhythm.

## Working system strategy

1. Record inherited tokens and shell behavior.
2. Design one representative focused page in context.
3. Extract a compact service-page extension system: page hero, local nav, evidence modules, content chapters, FAQ, CTA, responsive behavior.
4. Apply it flexibly to four more focused pages.
5. Extend it for the European Repair hub without forcing the short-page layout onto it.
