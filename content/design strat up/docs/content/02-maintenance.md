How Often Should You Service Your Car?
As a general guideline, we recommend changing your oil every 5,000 miles or every 6
months, depending on your driving habits. We also suggest checking your other fluids, tires,
and wear items at those same intervals so small issues get caught before they turn into
larger ones. Your vehicle’s owner’s manual may specify its own schedule, and we’re happy to
help you follow it.
