Electrical faults can be tricky to track down because a single symptom, such as a dead
battery, a flickering gauge, or an unresponsive screen, can have several possible causes. We
take the time to diagnose the root of the problem rather than just treating the symptom, so
you’re not back in the shop for the same issue a month later.

Frequently Asked Questions
Why does my dashboard warning light keep coming on?

Warning lights can point to anything from a sensor fault to a genuine mechanical issue. We’ll
run diagnostics to find the real cause and explain it clearly before any repair.

Can you replace my car battery?

Yes. We test and replace batteries and can check the charging system at the same time to
make sure a weak alternator isn’t the underlying problem.

Do you fix audio and interior electronics?

We do, from audio systems to interior electronic components. Bring it in and we’ll diagnose
the issue.

Can you perform software updates on my vehicle?

Yes, we handle software updates as part of our electrical and electronics services, including
on European models.
