Frequently Asked Questions
Do you do auto body work onsite?

We don’t have an onsite auto body facility, but we coordinate the work through three trusted
partner shops and handle drop-off and pickup for you here at Acton Autowerks.
Can you help after a collision?

Yes. We assist with written estimates, drop-off and pickup, and coordinating the repair so
you have one point of contact through the process.

Do you handle classic and vintage European car restoration?

We do, through two partner restoration shops, from full frame-off restorations to smaller
structural details. Reach out and we’ll talk through what your project needs.

What is paintless dent removal?

It’s a method of removing minor dents and dings without repainting, which helps preserve
your vehicle’s original finish. Ask us whether your dent is a good candidate.
